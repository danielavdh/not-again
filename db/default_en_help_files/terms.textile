Before you continue: this installation stores your bookkeeping data — accounts, transactions,
receipts, and any personal data of other people that passes through them.

h2. Who can see it

The administrators of this installation with top-level access can open any set of books on it,
including yours. That is inherent to how self-hosting works, not a choice this particular
installation makes: whoever runs the server is always the one person for whom there is no
technical way to keep the data private.

h2. Your own account

Your username, email address, and a record of your sign-ins — including failed ones — are kept
for security. That stays on this installation; it is not shared anywhere else.

h2. How long records are kept

Bookkeeping records are kept for as long as your access is active, and afterwards for as long as
the law that applies to your business requires — then deleted. See
"the full privacy notice":/legal for the detail.

This is a short summary of how this installation handles your data, not a full legal contract.
Agreeing confirms you were told this before you continued.
