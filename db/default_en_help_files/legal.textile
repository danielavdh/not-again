h2. What personal data we process

* *Account details* — your name, email address and a securely hashed password.

* *Bookkeeping records you enter* — accounts, transactions, journal entries and bank entries, including amounts, dates and descriptions.

* *Documents you upload* — receipts and supporting files.

* *Tax data* — the income and expense figures and filing obligations used to submit Making Tax Digital (MTD) returns to HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC).

* *HMRC authorisation* — the OAuth access tokens that let us file on your behalf once you have authorised us with HMRC.

* *Technical data* — the cookies listed below, and the fraud-prevention data HMRC requires us to send with each submission.

h2. Why we process it, and our lawful basis

We process your data for two purposes only:

* *To provide the bookkeeping and filing service you signed up for.* Lawful basis: performance of a contract.

* *To meet Making Tax Digital filing duties and to keep the tax records the law requires.* Lawful basis: compliance with a legal obligation.

We do not process your data for advertising, and we do not sell it or share it with third parties for marketing.

h2. Sharing your data with HMRC

When you file a return, we transmit the relevant income and expense figures, together with the fraud-prevention data HMRC requires, to HM Revenue & Customs through their MTD APIs, on your instruction. HMRC is a separate data controller for the information it receives.

h2. Where your data is stored

All of your data is stored within the European Union:

* The application and database are hosted with Hetzner in Germany.

* Uploaded files, receipts, tax filings and your books archive are stored with Scaleway Object Storage in Paris, France.

* A nightly copy of everything, including database backups, is kept with OVH Object Storage in Strasbourg, France, for redundancy across two providers.

* Outgoing email — including this policy's own transactional mail — is sent through Brevo.

h2. How long we keep it

We keep your records for as long as your account is active, and for as long afterwards as the applicable law requires them to be retained. Record-keeping periods differ between countries and tax regimes, so we retain your data for whatever period the law that applies to you requires. When data is no longer required and there is no legal obligation to keep it, it is deleted.
